## New worked examples and common mistakes (drafts)

Every Lesson 2 part has a worked example and a common mistake, and the lesson page's step reveal and spot-the-mistake quiz use them. The split left some parts without one, so these were drafted:

- **L1-1 common mistake:** |-5| = -5 is wrong. Absolute value is a distance from zero, so it is never negative. Correct: |-5| = 5.
- **L1-2 worked example, "Ordering integers":** Order 4, -6, 0, and -1 from least to greatest. Step 1: Place each number on the number line. -6 is farthest left and 4 is farthest right. Step 2: Between them, -1 is left of 0. Step 3: Read the numbers from left to right. Answer: -6, -1, 0, 4
- **L3-2 worked example, "Modeling a change":** A diver is at -12 m and rises 5 m. Where is the diver now? Step 1: The start is -12. Rises means a positive change: +5. Step 2: Add: -12 + 5 = -7. Step 3: Interpret: the diver is 7 m below sea level. Answer: -7 m (7 m below sea level)
- **L3-2 common mistake:** Writing a fall of 6 degrees as +6 is wrong. Fall means a negative change, so write -6.
