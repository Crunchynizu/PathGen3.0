Enable Study Access

Students will be able to start and resume learning sessions.

Only students are gated by this setting. Teachers and admins are unaffected.

Recorded in the audit log. Visible to admins only.

Disable Study Access

Students will be blocked from starting or resuming any session.

All active student sessions will fail closed. Scores, BKT, and path state are untouched.

Recorded in the audit log.

Enable Post-test Access

Eligible students may begin the post-test. Ineligible students remain locked.

Both this setting and path state posttest_ready are required. Enabling alone does not unlock every student.

Recorded in the audit log.

Disable Post-test Access

Students will see the post-test as a locked step.

Earlier content remains reviewable. Only the post-test is locked.

Recorded in the audit log.
